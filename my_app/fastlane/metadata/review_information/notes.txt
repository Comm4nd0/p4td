About this app

Paws 4 Thought Dogs is the app for a UK dog daycare and boarding business operating in Berkshire and Buckinghamshire. Dog owners use it to follow their dog's day, manage schedules and request boarding. The same app also serves the business's own staff, who use it to run the daily pickup route and assignments.

The demo account

The account supplied with this submission is a customer (dog owner) account. It has one dog with a complete profile, a populated photo feed and an upcoming schedule, so every owner-facing screen can be reviewed end to end.
Staff features are permission-gated and are NOT visible to this account. Daily assignments, the staff Day Board, request approvals, traffic alerts, team availability and dog notes are each controlled by a per-user staff permission granted by the business, so they do not appear for a customer account even though the App Store description lists them.
If you would like a staff-level account in order to review those screens, please ask via Resolution Center and we will provide one promptly.

Accounts and deletion

Anyone can create an account from the app's landing screen or the sign-in screen, via Register, which includes accepting the privacy policy.
A newly created account has no dog and no feed content until the business approves the dog's details, so a fresh registration will look empty. That is why the demo account above is supplied for review rather than asking you to register.
Accounts can be deleted from inside the app, under Profile.

What is new in this version

This release is mostly staff-side, so most of it is not reachable from the customer demo account. Listed here so it is clear what changed rather than as a request to test it: the staff app now works offline with a cached route and dog details and an automatic refresh when signal returns; the Day Board gained pinch-to-zoom and a card-size toggle; dog profiles show a map of the pickup address; a boarding stay can be cancelled from a dog's profile; and staff can share several feed photos at once.
The customer-visible changes are the floating quick-actions menu, and a fix to how cached data is separated between accounts when more than one account is used on the same device.

Other things worth knowing

Push notifications are used for feed updates, booking confirmations, and traffic alerts affecting a pickup or drop-off.
Camera and photo access is requested only when a user chooses to upload a picture of their dog.
The app talks only to the operator's own backend. There are no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and no use of the advertising identifier.

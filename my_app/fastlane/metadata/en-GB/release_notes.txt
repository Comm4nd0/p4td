This update is for the Paws 4 Thought team. Nothing changes for clients.

For staff:
• Day off requests are easier to deal with. Managers get a notification when someone asks for time off, and the request shows in Action Items on the dashboard, on My Availability in the menu and at the top of the Time Off tab.
• You get a notification when your day off request is approved or declined. Tapping it opens Time Off.
• Assigning dogs to someone who is off that day now asks first. You can cancel, or choose Assign anyway.

Clearer days, for owners and the team

• Your dog's calendar now shows every day they're booked in, including days the team has added for them, and you can scroll back over the days they've already been in.
• A reminder the evening before if your dog has an extra day booked or isn't coming in on one of their usual days.

For staff:
• A 6pm summary of tomorrow's changes to the routine (extra days, days off, transport and boarding), with your own run first.
• Late changes are pushed to the day's team straight away, shown on the dashboard's Changes card, and confirmed with Got it. Schedule alerts can be switched off from your Profile.
• Extra day and Transport changed chips on the board, and "not today" cards on a driver's list.
• Compatibility notes can now cover a group of dogs, flagged only on days they're all in together.

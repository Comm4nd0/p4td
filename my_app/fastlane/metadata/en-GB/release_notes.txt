Vaccination certificates

Attach the vet's certificate — a PDF or a photo — to your dog's profile, from the Vaccinations screen or straight from the dashboard. Certificates are private to the dog's owners and the team. We need one on file for our licence records, so until your dog has a current certificate the app says so on the dog's card and under Needs Your Attention, and sends the odd reminder that opens the attach screen for you. A new dog's certificate is asked for on the booking form. When a dog's last vaccination date is coming up to a year old, you get a reminder a week before.

Link My Dog

If your dog already comes to daycare, choose Link My Dog instead of the new-dog form: give the dog's name and the postcode and phone number we hold, and the team attaches the dog to your account.

Notifications you were missing

The app now tells you when your dog has been collected and when they're home, when the team replies to a message, and when a new photo of your dog lands on the feed. Each has its own switch on your Profile page.

Your Dashboard

A Dashboard tab of your own: where each dog is today and when they're next in, a calendar of all your dogs by day, week or month, anything that needs your attention (an unpaid invoice, a vaccination due, a missing certificate, a request waiting on us, a reply from the team), your latest invoice, upcoming boarding, the newest photos of your dogs and any closures coming up, with Quick Actions for changing days, requesting boarding and contacting staff.

Privacy

Other clients see you by your first name only, the feed opens once your booking form has been approved, and your dog's gallery is private to you and the team. Comments on the shared feed are seen by every client, and the comment box now says so.

Invoices

Invoices list one line per day your dog was in, so you can see exactly which days you're paying for. They're paid by bank transfer from the invoice Xero emails you, so the Pay Now button has gone.

Your details

Change the email you sign in with from your Profile. Pickup instructions (keys, gates, where the dog waits) now belong to each dog rather than your account, so two dogs at one address can differ. A contact number and an emergency contact are required on the booking form and on each dog.

For the team

Invoicing bills in advance: a month's invoice charges the days a dog is booked in, plus any unbilled extra days from last month. Daycare is priced by how many days a week the dog comes, with a per-dog override for payment managers. An invoice can be raised for a single dog and lands in Xero as a draft.

The staff dashboard shows how many dogs are dropped off and collected by their owners each day, red until someone is put in charge of meeting them, and spotlights whichever of these still needs doing. The incompatible-dogs warning covers every pair in on the same day and can be acknowledged for the whole team. The Dog health row lists unneutered males, vaccination dates over a year old and dogs with no current certificate, each with a one-tap Remind. A vehicle's MOT and service can be marked done. Resolved Contact Staff threads stay out of the Open list.

Contact Staff messages, booking forms, website enquiries and requests each have their own icon and count at the top of the staff home screen; a count clears only once the item has been dealt with. A business-wide activity log records who did what and when, with each dog's entries on its profile. Requests to add a day show who is already booked and who is on duty, Reassign Dogs moves several dogs to another driver at once, owners are shown by full name throughout, and payment managers can see which invoice covered any day on a dog's calendar.

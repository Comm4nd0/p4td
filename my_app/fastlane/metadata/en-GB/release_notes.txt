See your bookings in your own calendar

• New: add your dogs' daycare and boarding days to Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar. Tap Add to my calendar under the Calendar on your dashboard, and it keeps itself up to date.

Clearer calendars on your dog's profile

• Changes now show on your dog's calendar straight away, with no need to close and reopen the app.
• Cancelled days are marked with a cross, and Cancelled has been added to the calendar key.
• When a day is moved, the old date shows as cancelled and the new date as an extra day.

For staff:
• Pins on the pick-up map now show the dog's initial instead of a paw, so you can see at a glance which dog it is.

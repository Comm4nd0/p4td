Notifications now take you to the right place.

• Tapping a notification opens what it is about, including when the app was closed. Before, the app could open on your Dashboard with nothing about the notification on it.
• A new photo or comment notification opens the feed with that post at the top.
• An extra day or day off reminder opens your calendar on that day. So does a waitlist space opening up.
• A notification about a profile change or a new photo in your dog's gallery opens your dog's profile.

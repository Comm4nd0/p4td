App Lock

You can now lock the app behind Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Turn it on in your profile.
Once it's on, the app asks to unlock when you open it, and again if it has been in the background for more than a minute — so switching out to your camera or maps doesn't get in your way.
Your login is remembered exactly as before; the lock sits over it.
Roadworks on Your Route

The staff dashboard now flags roadworks in force on the day, already matched to the routes and dogs they disrupt.
A staff member's card is ringed when their route is affected, their dog list carries a banner naming the roadworks, and the pickup map marks each one with a cone pin.
Weekend Boarding

The staff dashboard now covers Saturdays and Sundays, so you can see which dogs are staying on any night of the weekend. Daycare is Monday to Friday, so weekend days show boarding only.
Every boarding entry now shows the dates the stay runs from and to, so a dog going home in the morning reads differently from one staying another week.
Site Defects is now reachable from the menu.

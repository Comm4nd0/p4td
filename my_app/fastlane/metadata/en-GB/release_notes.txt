Incident Log

Staff can now write up an incident — a scuffle, an injury, a dog getting loose — naming every dog involved, what happened, any injuries, and what was done about it, with photos or video attached at the time or added later.
Each dog on an incident carries its own account, because two dogs in the same scuffle rarely come out of it the same way, and every dog's profile links through to the incidents it has been involved in.
Incidents are staff only. They are never shown to owners in the app — what an owner is told is a conversation, and staff tick it off per dog as they make it.

Boarding Dogs on the Daycare Roster

A dog that is boarding is now booked into daycare for every weekday of its stay, arrival and going-home day included, so it turns up on the day's roster and headcount rather than only in the boarding list.
Nothing changes about what a stay costs: the nights are still the only charge.

Elsewhere

Uploading to the feed has moved off the dashboard and onto the feed page, where the post actually lands.

Now on iPad and Mac

The app now runs properly on iPad — and through it on Apple silicon Macs — rather than as a scaled-up phone screen. On a big screen the menu stays open at the side wherever you go: pages like Contact Staff and Booking Forms open beside it instead of covering it, with a back button to step out of deeper pages as usual.

Made for bigger screens

Dogs are laid out as photo tiles rather than one long stretched column, the staff dashboard puts the day's roster and the action list side by side, and the day board opens with every staff column fitted on screen. Photo grids gain columns instead of blowing up, and forms, lists and sheets keep a comfortable reading width. Nothing changes on the phone.

Also in this release

The staff overview's status lines no longer overflow on smaller phones.

Changing a boarding stay, and smarter pick-up runs

• Need different dates for an approved boarding stay? You can now ask to change them from the booking. The team will confirm, and your stay keeps its current dates until they do.
• Add up to five other addresses for your dog (Grandma's, a dog walker's) under Other addresses on their profile, so the team can collect from or drop off there on the days you need.

For staff:
• Boarding managers can review those date-change requests, and edit any boarding booking from the dog's profile or from the boarding dogs on the dashboard.
• Plan Routes on the dashboard shares the day's dogs between the drivers you choose, and Optimise route puts a driver's run in the best order. Nothing changes until you accept the plan.
• Choose one of a dog's other addresses for the day's pick-up, drop-off or both from the dog's card with Pick-up / drop-off address.
• A dog whose owner wants them home after 15:30 or after 16:00 now shows it in bold on the driver's pick-up list.
• Open a dog straight from the day's Changes vs normal list.
• Fixed days appearing twice around the clock change.

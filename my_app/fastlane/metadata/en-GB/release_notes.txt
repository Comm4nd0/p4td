Works Offline

The staff dashboard and dog details now load instantly from saved data and refresh in the background, so the app keeps working when signal drops mid-route.
Your route and today's dog photos are prefetched when you log in, so everything is ready before you set off.
An amber banner tells you when you're looking at saved data, and what time it was saved.
The app now watches for signal returning and refreshes on its own.
Staff Day Board

Pinch to zoom the day board, plus a new card-size toggle for denser or larger cards.
The board now always opens fully zoomed out, fitting the most staff and dogs on screen.
Swapping staff now moves dogs that have already been picked up, instead of reporting nothing swapped.
Fixed “Remove from this day” leaving the dog in the unassigned list.
Fixed re-added days not showing after a dog had been removed from a day.
Dog Profiles & Boarding

Dog profiles now show a map of the pickup address; tap it to open the location in your maps app.
Staff can cancel a boarding stay directly from a dog's profile.
Sharing

New Share to Socials quick action: choose several feed photos and post them together to Facebook, Instagram or any other installed app. The order you pick them becomes the photo order.
Getting Around

Dashboard quick actions and dog profile buttons are now a floating speed dial, anchored to the button it opens from.
Reliability & Privacy

Fixed a caching problem that could show a previous account's dogs after adding a second account.
A failed check no longer looks like a clean day: if unassigned dogs or compatibility conflicts can't be loaded, you now get a retryable warning instead of silence.
Drivers are no longer shown an hours-old route as though it were live.

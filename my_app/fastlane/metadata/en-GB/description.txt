Stay connected with your dog's daycare experience with the Paws 4 Thought Dogs app — the easiest way to manage your dog's schedule, view their daily adventures, and communicate with the team.

For Dog Owners:

• Photo & Video Feed — See photos and videos of your dog enjoying their day at daycare. React and comment on posts from staff.

• Schedule Management — View your dog's daycare days, request date changes, and book additional sessions with a tap.

• Boarding Requests — Need overnight care? Submit boarding requests with special instructions and track approval status.

• Dog Profiles — Manage your dog's details including food instructions, medical notes, and preferred pickup times.

• Direct Communication — Message the team with questions or concerns and get replies straight to your phone.

• Push Notifications — Get notified about feed updates, booking confirmations, and traffic alerts affecting pickup or drop-off.

• Holiday Calendar — Check upcoming closure dates so you can plan ahead.

For Staff:

• Daily Assignments — See which dogs are in each day, assign them to staff members, and track status from pickup through drop-off.

• Request Management — Review and approve schedule changes and boarding requests.

• Traffic Alerts — Quickly notify owners of delays affecting their pickup or drop-off route.

• Team Availability — Manage your own schedule and, for managers, view team coverage at a glance.

• Dog Notes — Record compatibility, behavioural, and grouping notes to keep the team informed.

Paws 4 Thought Dogs is a professional dog daycare service based in the UK. Download the app to get started — all you need is your account details from the team.

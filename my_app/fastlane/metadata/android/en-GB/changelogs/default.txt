Vaccination certificates: attach the vet's certificate from your dog's profile or the dashboard, and the app reminds you until one is on file. New dogs are asked for it on the booking form. Link My Dog attaches a dog that already comes to daycare to your account. Your dashboard now has a calendar of all your dogs by day, week or month. For the team: certificate reminders from the Dog health row, a Mark done tick for MOT and service, and the dashboard spotlights what needs doing now.
